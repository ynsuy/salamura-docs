Delete your account

You can initiate permanent deletion directly in salamura:

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Under Account and legal, choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm permanent deletion.

Deleting your account removes access to synchronized settings and learning progress associated with it. Subscription cancellation is managed separately by Apple App Store or Google Play; deleting the salamura account does not automatically cancel an active store subscription.

If the app asks you to authenticate again or you cannot access your account, use the support page.
