Privacy Policy

Effective July 22, 2026

salamura uses the minimum information needed to provide accounts, synchronize learning progress, deliver reminders, and manage optional Pro subscriptions.

Information we process

  • Account information such as your email address and Firebase user identifier.
  • Learning settings and progress, including language pairs, answers, scores, review schedules, story progress, and grammar progress.
  • Purchase status and subscription entitlement supplied by the app store and RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Notification preferences stored on your device and, where applicable, technical diagnostics needed to operate the app.

Services

Firebase provides authentication and progress synchronization. RevenueCat and Apple App Store or Google Play process subscriptions and determine Pro access. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and progress information while your account is active. You can start account deletion inside the app under Profile → Account and legal → Delete account. See the account deletion page for help.

Your choices

You may disable notifications in device settings, restore purchases from the app, and request deletion of your account. Questions can be submitted through the support page.
